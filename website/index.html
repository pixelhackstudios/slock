SLOCK

Tilt the world to slide a jelly block through a maze that never ends.

Download free

Windows, Mac and Linux

You don't move the block. You tilt the world.

  • Tilt a little and it creeps.
  • Tilt hard and it slides.
  • Level the board and it stops.

It runs along the corridors and stops right on a tile. Lean toward a side opening, even a moment early, and it turns in at speed.

Eat the blue ones. Beat the clock.

You start with 35 seconds, and every pellet buys more. Clear the blue pellets, or find the key in the side room, and the gate at the far end opens.

Bonus

Mind the Swurms.

Green jelly worms crawl out of the pen in the middle, one at a time, and every section has one more. Touch one and it steals a third of your time.

Grab a big gold pellet and they turn blue for ten seconds. Now you eat them, and get your time back.

Powerups

They work the moment you touch one, and another turns up ten seconds later somewhere you've already cleared.

  1. Three blue dots

    A quarter of the pellets vanish.

  2. Steel block

    Slock of Steel: smash through walls and Swurms for ten seconds.

  3. Three slugs

    Three more slugs.

  4. One big slug

    One more slug.

  5. Green patch

    Every hole fills in and every gap in the walls closes.

  6. Little red Slock

    One more life. Hoard them.

Slugs: click to aim, click again to fire. Blast a Swurm, or knock a hole through a wall.

It keeps going up.

Every gate leads up to a new section. The blocks get smaller, the mazes get denser, and there's another Swurm.

You have three lives. Fall off and you're back at that section's gate, with everything just as you left it.

Make the top ten and your name goes on the leaderboard.
